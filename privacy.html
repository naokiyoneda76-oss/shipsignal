ShipSignal — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

ShipSignal is a Jira Cloud app built on Atlassian Forge. It computes release-risk assessments from Jira issue data and presents them in an issue panel and through a Rovo agent.

What data the app accesses

The app reads Jira issue data (such as summaries, statuses, priorities, due dates, and fix versions) and the site's priority list, using the Forge platform's app identity, only when a user opens the ShipSignal panel or invokes one of its agent actions.

What data the app stores

None. ShipSignal does not store, persist, or transmit any data outside the Atlassian site it is installed on. Assessments are computed on demand and displayed; nothing is written to any database, file, or external service. The app makes no network calls to third-party servers.

Comments and tasks created by the app

When a user explicitly invokes the "post brief" or "create blocker tasks" actions, the app writes a comment or creates issues inside the same Jira site, at the user's request. That content lives in Jira under the site's own data policies.
